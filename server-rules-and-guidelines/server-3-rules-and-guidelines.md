# Server 3 Rules & Guidelines

## FivePD Server Rules

_End of Watch Roleplay_

These rules apply when participating in **FivePD patrol sessions**. FivePD focuses on cooperative law enforcement gameplay where players work together to respond to calls and incidents.

Players are expected to maintain **professional, cooperative, and realistic behavior** while participating in FivePD sessions.

***

### 1. Cooperative Gameplay

FivePD is designed to be played cooperatively.

Players must:

* Work together with other officers
* Share calls and assist other units when needed
* Avoid taking every call alone

{% hint style="info" %}
FivePD sessions are meant to simulate a team-based patrol environment.
{% endhint %}

***

### 2. Follow Callout Structure

When responding to FivePD callouts:

* Respond to calls in a **controlled and organized manner**
* Do not rush every call immediately
* Allow multiple units to respond when appropriate

Avoid turning every call into a **solo situation**.

***

### 3. Proper Use of Emergency Vehicles

Emergency vehicles must be used responsibly.

Players should:

* Use lights and sirens appropriately
* Drive realistically when responding to calls
* Avoid reckless driving that would cause unnecessary crashes

Reckless driving may result in removal from patrol.

***

### 4. Professional Conduct

Players are expected to behave like **professional law enforcement officers**.

This includes:

* Using respectful communication
* Avoiding trolling or unrealistic behavior
* Following the direction of supervisors if present

***

### 5. Scene Control

During active incidents:

* The first responding officer should begin scene control
* Additional units should assist rather than take over the scene
* Avoid talking over each other during important situations

Maintaining order helps keep situations **organized and realistic**.

***

### 6. Use of Force

Use of force must remain **reasonable and appropriate**.

Officers should:

* Attempt verbal commands when possible
* Use non-lethal options when appropriate
* Avoid unnecessary lethal force

Every situation should be handled with **proper escalation**.

***

### 7. No Callout Abuse

Players may not abuse or manipulate callout systems.

Examples include:

* Intentionally triggering multiple callouts repeatedly
* Ignoring active calls
* Forcing callouts to create chaos

Callouts exist to provide **structured gameplay scenarios**.

***

### 8. Do Not Disrupt Active Calls

Players not involved in a call should avoid interfering.

Do not:

* Interrupt active scenes
* Take over another officer's call
* Crowd scenes unnecessarily

If multiple units are already handling a call, continue normal patrol.

***

### 9. Communication

Clear communication is important during FivePD patrols.

Players should:

* Communicate when responding to calls
* Inform others when they need backup
* Keep voice communications clear and professional

***

### 10. Staff Instructions

Staff members may intervene during patrol sessions if needed.

Players must:

* Follow staff instructions
* Avoid arguing during active gameplay
* Address concerns after the session if needed

***

## Patrol Expectations

The goal of FivePD gameplay is to create a **structured and enjoyable law enforcement experience** where players work together to respond to calls, investigate incidents, and maintain order.

{% hint style="info" %}
FivePD patrols should focus on teamwork, realism, and cooperative gameplay.
{% endhint %}
