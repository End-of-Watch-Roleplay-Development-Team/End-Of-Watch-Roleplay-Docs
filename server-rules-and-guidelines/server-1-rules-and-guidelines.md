# Server 1 Rules & Guidelines

## General Server Rules

_End of Watch Roleplay_

These rules apply to **all members** of the server regardless of department, role, or rank. The purpose of these rules is to maintain a **fair, respectful, and immersive roleplay environment** for everyone.

{% hint style="info" %}
Failure to follow these rules may result in warnings, removals from scenes, suspensions, or permanent bans depending on severity.
{% endhint %}

***

### 1. Respect All Members

All players must treat others with respect both **in-character (IC)** and **out-of-character (OOC)**.

The following behavior is not allowed:

* Harassment or bullying
* Discriminatory language
* Personal attacks or insults
* Intentionally provoking other players

Disagreements should be handled **calmly and respectfully**.

***

### 2. Roleplay First

This server exists to provide **quality roleplay**. All actions should contribute to the roleplay environment.

Players should:

* Prioritize storytelling and interaction
* Avoid behavior that ruins immersion
* Create scenarios that others can participate in

If an action only benefits you and harms the RP experience for others, it likely should not be done.

***

### 3. No Fail Roleplay (FailRP)

FailRP occurs when a player acts in a way that **breaks realism or ignores the situation they are in**.

Examples include:

* Ignoring injuries after serious crashes or shootings
* Acting without regard for your character’s safety
* Escaping situations in unrealistic ways

Always act in a way that **makes sense for the situation**.

***

### 4. No Metagaming

Metagaming is the use of **out-of-character information within roleplay**.

Examples include:

* Using information from Discord, Twitch streams, or chats in RP
* Acting on information your character would not realistically know
* Sharing information between characters without roleplay interaction

Information learned outside of roleplay **cannot be used in-character**.

***

### 5. No Powergaming

Powergaming involves forcing roleplay actions on others **without giving them a chance to respond**.

Examples include:

* Forcing outcomes in `/me` actions
* Declaring actions that remove another player's ability to react
* Controlling another player's character

Roleplay should always allow **both sides to participate fairly**.

***

### 6. Proper Use of Voice Chat

Voice communication should remain **appropriate and relevant**.

* Avoid excessive yelling or microphone spam
* Do not play loud music or soundboards during RP
* Keep OOC discussion minimal during active roleplay

Voice chat should **enhance roleplay, not disrupt it**.

***

### 7. No Exploiting

Exploiting bugs, glitches, or server systems is strictly prohibited.

Examples include:

* Using bugs to escape law enforcement
* Duplication of items or money
* Abusing mechanics for unfair advantages

Any discovered exploit must be reported to staff immediately.

***

### 8. Staff Respect

Staff members are responsible for maintaining order and fairness.

Players must:

* Follow staff instructions during active situations
* Avoid arguing with staff during roleplay scenes
* Bring concerns to staff **after the situation has ended**

Staff decisions made during scenes are **final at that moment**.

***

### 9. No Advertising

Advertising other communities without permission is not allowed.

This includes:

* Discord servers
* Other FiveM communities
* External services or promotions

Permission must be granted by **server leadership**.

***

### 10. Use Common Sense

Not every situation can be covered by written rules.

Players are expected to use **good judgment and common sense** at all times.

If an action would clearly:

* Ruin roleplay
* Create unfair situations
* Negatively impact other players

Then it likely **should not be done**.

***

## Rule Enforcement

Breaking these rules may result in administrative action including:

* Verbal or written warnings
* Removal from roleplay scenes
* Temporary suspension
* Permanent bans

Administrative actions are taken to protect the roleplay experience for the entire community.
