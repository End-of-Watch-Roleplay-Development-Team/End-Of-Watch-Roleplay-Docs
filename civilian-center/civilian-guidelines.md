# Civilian Guidelines

## Civilian Guidelines

_End of Watch Roleplay_

These guidelines exist to ensure civilian roleplay remains **fun, realistic, and fair** for everyone involved. Civilians play a huge role in creating interesting scenarios for law enforcement, fire, and other players.

***

### 1. Realistic Roleplay

Civilian actions should remain believable.

* Avoid unrealistic behavior that would never happen in real life.
* Your goal is to **create RP scenarios**, not simply cause chaos.
* If your actions constantly disrupt roleplay, staff may intervene.

**Examples of unrealistic behavior:**

* Randomly committing crimes with no RP reason
* Running from police for no reason
* Intentionally causing massive destruction without RP buildup

***

### 2. Value Your Life

Your character should act like their life matters.

* Do not act fearless when faced with weapons or dangerous situations.
* If you are clearly outnumbered or outgunned, comply with the situation.
* Suicide-by-cop behavior is not allowed.

***

### 3. Crime Limitations

Crime should create roleplay, not chaos.

* Large or serious crimes should have **proper roleplay buildup**.
* Avoid repeating the same crime back-to-back.
* Allow law enforcement time to reset between major incidents.

Staff may limit crime if it becomes excessive.

***

### 4. Vehicle Roleplay

Vehicles should be used realistically.

* No vehicle ramming unless it makes sense in the scenario.
* Do not intentionally destroy vehicles without RP reason.
* Unrealistic driving (constant off-roading in supercars, etc.) should be avoided.

***

### 5. Traffic Stops

When stopped by law enforcement:

* Pull over safely and promptly.
* Remain in the vehicle unless instructed otherwise.
* Do not immediately escalate situations.

Traffic stops are **RP opportunities**, not instant pursuits.

***

### 6. Criminal Escalation

Not every situation needs to become violent.

* Start small and escalate naturally if needed.
* Violence should be a **last resort**, not the first option.
* Avoid turning every police interaction into a shootout.

***

### 7. Scenario Variety

Civilians should try to create **different types of scenarios**.

Examples of good civilian roleplay:

* Running a business
* Roadside assistance
* Car meets
* Construction zones
* Public events or gatherings
* Minor criminal activity
* Suspicious person or vehicle calls

The goal is to keep RP **dynamic and interesting**.

***

### 8. No Intentional Disruption

Do not intentionally ruin roleplay.

Examples include:

* Trolling
* Interfering with active scenes
* Blocking emergency services
* Interrupting serious RP moments

***

### 9. Follow Server Systems

If the server uses economy or framework systems, they must be respected.

* Do not exploit mechanics.
* Use civilian jobs and systems properly.
* Any discovered exploit must be reported to staff.

***

### 10. Listen to Staff

Staff decisions during RP are final.

* If staff asks you to stop or adjust a scenario, comply.
* Disputes should be handled **after the RP scene**, not during it.

***

### 11. Common Sense Rule

If something seems like it would ruin roleplay or be unrealistic, **do not do it**.

Staff may take action on situations not specifically listed in these guidelines.

***

### Goal of Civilian Roleplay

The goal of civilian roleplay is to create immersive scenarios that give **everyone something fun to participate in**, not just yourself.
